Yuhui: Learning & AI Creation

Yuhui
Consumer AppAI CreationInteraction Design

Project Background

Yuhui brings learning and AI creation into one mobile experience. As AI tools enter education, users still struggle to write prompts, trust generated results and move smoothly through the creative process.

Understanding Learning Needs

Research highlighted fragmented learning paths and limited time for discovery. Students need clearer guidance, teachers face time-consuming feedback, and AI creators need a more continuous path from generation to refinement.

Connecting Learning & Creation

Five areas — Home, Courses, AI, Messages and Profile — connect learning resources, creative tools and collaboration. The structure brings everyday campus tasks and AI-assisted work into a shared journey.

Interaction: Reach & Hierarchy

Primary actions sit within natural thumb reach to support one-handed use. Key information follows an F-shaped scanning pattern, helping users identify important content quickly.

AI Creation & Visual Identity

AI dialogue, image creation, assignment feedback and document tools support different learning tasks. A shared character and visual language connect these features across the app.

Project Reflection

AI experiences need to reduce uncertainty, not simply demonstrate capability. Users should understand why a result appears and how to improve it. Further work would strengthen feedback and classroom collaboration.
