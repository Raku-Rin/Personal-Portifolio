Cangjingyou: Discover & Plan

Cangjingyou
Consumer AppTravel ExperienceUI / UX Design

Project Background

Cangjingyou helps travellers feel a destination before they arrive, then turn inspiration into a practical itinerary. The design focuses on atmosphere, destination information and saved routes, balancing emotional appeal with everyday usability.

From Inspiration to Decisions

Interviews and competitor research showed users moving repeatedly between inspiring content and practical routes. The experience needs to connect cultural discovery, location-aware guidance and itinerary planning without breaking that flow.

A Connected Travel Journey

Home, Itinerary, Discover and Profile organise cultural exploration, planning and local interaction. Together, they connect destination discovery with route decisions and services during the trip.

Components: Consistent & Useful

Reusable cards bring destination details, weather, schedules and saved places into a consistent visual system. These elements support the transition from browsing to planning across the app.

Onboarding & Interface Design

The introduction presents itinerary planning, travel assistance and community discovery. A shared character and warm visual language connect these entry points with the wider travel experience.

Project Reflection

Travel experiences need both an emotional rhythm and a clear structure: users are shaping a desire to go and a plan they can follow. Next steps would refine route recommendations and integrate preparation and altitude guidance into saved itineraries.
